A season of feeding your baby, held with people doing the same thing at the
same time. Each meeting starts with what has come up during the week, and moves
into one part of feeding in depth — with a lactation consultant in the room.

You do not need to arrive with a problem, and you do not need to have it
figured out. Bring the week you have had.
